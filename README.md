# SimplySpectrum

Turn your phone's camera into a live spectrum analyzer. Point it at anything — a flower, a neon sign, your living room wall — and instantly see the rainbow of light it's made of.

## What It Does

**Live Spectrum Chart** — Watches the world through your camera and breaks it down into pure wavelengths (400–700 nm). You see a live histogram of which colors of light are actually present in the frame, with the rainbow bar underneath so you always know what you're looking at.

**Color Peak Detection** — Automatically finds and labels the most prominent color peaks in the spectrum. Up to 5 dominant wavelengths get marked right on the chart, so you can tell at a glance whether you're looking at a warm sunset (reds around 620 nm) or a cool LED (blues around 470 nm).

**Luminosity Meter** — A brightness histogram showing how light and dark are distributed across the frame, with an approximate lux readout. Great for checking how evenly lit a room or a photo setup is.

**Average Color** — Displays the overall average color of everything currently in view. Handy for matching paint, comparing fabric swatches, or just satisfying your curiosity about what color your desk "really" is.

**Extreme Light Spots** — Draws a black circle on the brightest area and a white circle on the darkest area of the camera view. Instantly see where the light is coming from and where the shadows fall.

**Color Enhancement** — Boosts saturation and contrast before analysis, helping the detector pull weak colors out of a washed-out scene. Toggle it on when the lighting is flat.

**Wavelength or Frequency** — Switch the spectrum axis between nanometers (nm) and terahertz (Hz) with one tap. Same data, two perspectives — handy for physics class or just feeling smart.

**Customizable Layout** — The main screen is a 2×2 grid: camera, spectrum chart, luminosity chart, and controls. Don't like where things are? Rearrange all four quadrants from Settings. Put the camera big on top, or tuck it into a corner — your call.

**Snapshot** — Capture the entire screen — charts, camera, everything — and save it to your gallery. Share your spectrum finds with friends, professors, or the internet.

**Torch & Camera Flip** — Built-in controls for the flash and front/back camera switching. No leaving the app, no fumbling with menus.

**Theme Support** — Light, dark, or follow the system. The camera feed always stays as-is; everything else adapts.

## Why You'll Want It

Ever held up a prism as a kid and watched white light split into a rainbow? This is that, but in your pocket, live, and way more precise. SimplySpectrum makes the invisible architecture of light visible in real time.

You'll start by pointing it at random things — and that's the fun. A green leaf isn't just "green." It's a spike at 550 nm. Your phone screen's blue glow? That's a sharp peak around 460 nm. An old-school incandescent bulb? A broad warm hill centered past 600 nm. Once you start seeing the world as wavelengths, you don't go back.

Photographers will love it for checking light balance. Teachers can use it for live demos without a lab. Students can actually see the electromagnetic spectrum they've only seen in textbooks. Designers and artists can explore how pigments and dyes reflect different parts of the spectrum. And anyone who's simply curious about the world will find themselves pulling it out to answer "what color is that, really?"

It's free, it's fast, and it turns the phone you already carry into a tool you never knew you wanted.
